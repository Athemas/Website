About

Jack Konijnendijk (also known as AudioJack) is a passionate sound designer, composer, and sound recorder with a love for creating immersive audio experiences. With a focus on crafting futuristic and otherworldly sounds, Jack utilizes synthesizers to bring unique auditory landscapes to life. Whether it's for films or other media, Jack's work is dedicated to delivering captivating and innovative soundscapes that enhance the overall experience.

Jack is currently in his third year of studying Film at the Dutch Filmers Academy, where he continues to hone his skills and expand his creative horizons.
