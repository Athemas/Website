Sound Design Projects

Alice

Short Film

Alice was my first short film that I have written, directed and edited. The sounddesign gives the feeling of passing time through evolving electronic textures and atmospheric layers. The composition uses synthesizers to create a futuristic and otherworldly feel, blending warm pads with subtle glitches to represent the protagonist's inner journey through a dreamlike landscape.

Neon Horizons

Animation

Neon Horizons is a vibrant animated piece exploring the intersection of technology and nature. The sound design features bright, crystalline synth melodies layered with organic field recordings, creating a unique contrast that reflects the duality of the visual narrative. Every sound was carefully crafted to complement the rhythm of the animation, building tension and resolution through frequency modulation and spatial effects.

Digital Echoes

Interactive Installation

Digital Echoes is an interactive audio installation that responds to user movement and environmental data. The soundscape consists of generative synthesizer patterns that evolve in real-time, creating a unique experience with each interaction. Deep bass foundations support ethereal pad textures, while algorithmic elements introduce elements of surprise and discovery, making the installation a living, breathing sonic environment.
